# LASUSTECH Computer Science SIWES Technical Report Quality Scorecard

## 1. Purpose and Philosophy
This scorecard objectively measures the quality of SIWES Technical Reports generated by Log2Def against the academic standards and expectations of supervising lecturers at the **Lagos State University of Science and Technology (LASUSTECH)**, Department of Computer Science.

The rubric acts as a rigorous quality gate in CI and production to prevent document degradation, prevent AI hallucinations, and ensure every student receives a report ready for immediate binding and oral defense.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **AUTOMATIC FAIL (0/100 · REJECTED)**:
> If any statement, activity, tool, technology, organisation name, or supervisor in the report is fabricated (i.e. cannot be grounded in the student's verified logbook or confirmed intake answers), or if any cross-project data leakage is detected, the report receives an **AUTOMATIC FAIL (Score: 0)** regardless of points earned in other categories.

---

## 2. 100-Point Scoring Rubric

The evaluation rubric assesses six distinct pillars totaling 100 points:

| Pillar | Category | Points | Automated vs AI Scored |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | **Structure Compliance** | 20 | Automated AST / Headings Check |
| 2 | **Content Specificity & Accuracy** | 25 | Grounded Evidence Verification + AI Reviewer |
| 3 | **Writing Quality** | 20 | Lexicon / Grammar / Voice / Non-duplication |
| 4 | **Presentation & Formatting** | 15 | OOXML / Layout Inspection |
| 5 | **Completeness & Length** | 10 | Real Render Page Count |
| 6 | **Authenticity** | 10 | AI Reviewer + Reflective Grounding |
| **Total** | | **100** | **Passing Benchmark: ≥ 90/100** |

---

### Pillar 1: Structure Compliance (20 Points)
Checks coordinator's approved Document B section hierarchy, front matter, numbering, and order:
- **Headings Verbatim & In Order (10 pts)**:
  All coordinator headings must match the approved template exactly:
  - Front matter: `Title Page`, `Certification`, `Dedication`, `Acknowledgement`, `Table of Contents`
  - Chapter 1: `1.1 Background of SIWES`, `1.2 Objectives of SIWES`, `1.3 Importance of SIWES`, `1.4 Scope of the Training`, `1.5 Duration of the Training`
  - Chapter 2: `2.1 Brief History of the Organisation`, `2.2 Objectives of the Organisation`, `2.3 Organisational Structure`, `2.4 Organogram of the Organisation`
  - Chapter 3: `3.1 Duties and Responsibilities`, `3.2 Activities Performed`, `3.3 Skills Acquired`, `3.4 Equipment/Tools Used`, `3.5 Challenges Encountered`, `3.6 Solutions to the Challenges`
  - Chapter 4: `4.1 Summary`, `4.2 Conclusion`, `4.3 Recommendations`
  - Back matter: Single heading `REFERENCES` (and optional `APPENDICES`).
  *Deductions*: -2 pts per missing or misnamed heading; -5 pts for out-of-order chapters.
- **Pagination Scheme (5 pts)**:
  Preliminary pages must use lowercase Roman numerals (`i` to `v`), starting with page `i` on Cover and `ii` on Certification. Main body must use Arabic numerals (`1` to `N`). Table of Contents must list dynamic real page numbers.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts for Roman numeral errors; -3 pts for stale or mismatched TOC page references.
- **Single References & Signatories (5 pts)**:
  Certification must contain the three required signature lines (`Industrial-Based Supervisor`, `SIWES Coordinator - Computer Science`, `HOD, Computer Science`). References must appear under a single heading without chapter prefixes.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts per missing signature block; -3 pts for improper References heading.

---

### Pillar 2: Content Specificity & Accuracy (25 Points)
Ensures technical depth, factual grounding, and total absence of hallucinations:
- **Strict Grounding & Zero Fabrication (10 pts)**:
  Every duty, weekly activity, skill, and tool must trace directly to the student's logbook records or confirmed profile.
  *Rule*: Any fabricated claim triggers **AUTOMATIC ZERO (FAIL)**.
- **Computer Science Relevance & Technical Depth (8 pts)**:
  Activities must reflect computing and IT competencies (e.g. software development, network protocols, database schemas, hardware diagnostics, containerisation, UI/UX engineering) rather than vague generic office tasks.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts per vague, vacuous, or unspecific cluster description; -4 pts if duties fail to articulate technical procedures.
- **Chronological & Project Coverage (7 pts)**:
  Section 3.2 must cover all verified logbook weeks in structured 5–7 week-range clusters (e.g. `WEEK 1-2: ...`, `WEEK 3-4: ...`). No verified week should be omitted, and detected gaps must be acknowledged.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts per omitted week-cluster; -3 pts if week blocks copy raw daily lines with 'Week N:' prefixes.

---

### Pillar 3: Writing Quality (20 Points)
Assesses academic prose, linguistic precision, voice, and flow:
- **British English & Terminology Consistency (5 pts)**:
  Enforces en-GB spelling across all sections (e.g. `organisation`, `specialising`, `colour`, `centre`, `behaviour`, `modularisation`, `program` for software vs `programme` for scheme).
  *Deductions*: -1 pt per American spelling instance (capped at -5 pts).
- **First-Person Active Perspective (5 pts)**:
  Student reflections and activities must use first-person past tense (`I developed`, `I diagnosed`, `I assisted`, `I configured`), avoiding third-person detachment (`The student did`) or inconsistent future tense.
  *Deductions*: -1 pt per third-person impersonal reference in body chapters.
- **Grammar, Syntax & Sentence Completeness (5 pts)**:
  Every sentence must be complete and grammatically sound with proper terminal punctuation. Zero placeholder markers (`[INPUT NEEDED]`, `TODO`, `lorem ipsum`).
  *Deductions*: -1 pt per sentence fragment; -5 pts for any placeholder or template leak.
- **Cross-Section Non-Duplication (5 pts)**:
  Prose in Section 4.1 (Summary) must not repeat sentences verbatim from Section 4.2 (Conclusion) or Chapter 3. Section 3.1 must summarize responsibilities by theme rather than copy Section 3.2 weekly prose.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts per verbatim duplicated sentence between sections.

---

### Pillar 4: Presentation & Formatting (15 Points)
Assesses visual and layout fidelity against university binding standards:
- **Typography & Font Integrity (4 pts)**:
  100% Times New Roman throughout: 14pt bold centered for chapter headings, 12pt bold left-aligned for subheadings, 12pt justified for body text with 1.5 line spacing.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts for non-Times New Roman fonts; -2 pts for wrong font sizes or body alignment not justified.
- **Margins & Spacing (4 pts)**:
  Exact 2.54 cm (1.0 inch) margins on all four sides. Chapter headings formatted with proper spacing before and after (12pt / 10pt); paragraph spacing 4pt after.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts for improper margins; -2 pts for excessive empty vertical gaps.
- **University Logo & Asset Integration (4 pts)**:
  Exactly one centered institution logo on the cover page (width ~3.2 cm, 1:1 aspect ratio, clean resolution). Logo must never appear on internal pages.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts if logo is missing; -4 pts if distorted or leaking onto non-cover pages.
- **Organogram Structure Chart (3 pts)**:
  Clean, high-resolution vector/image chart in Section 2.4 showing executive leadership, functional units, and student placement with supervisor attribution. Includes accessible text alternative.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts for raw ASCII art or missing chart; -1 pt for missing supervisor label.

---

### Pillar 5: Completeness & Length (10 Points)
Ensures the document satisfies the physical volume requirements:
- **Required Sections Completeness (5 pts)**:
  All mandatory preliminary, body, and conclusion sections must be present and substantiated.
  *Deductions*: -2 pts per missing required section.
- **12–15 Page Volume Calibration (5 pts)**:
  Total rendered document volume (from Cover to References/Appendices) must be within 12 to 15 pages in real render (PDF/DOCX).
  *Deductions*: -2 pts per page under 12 pages; -2 pts per page over 15 pages.

---

### Pillar 6: Authenticity (10 Points)
Evaluates whether the report sounds like an honest reflection of an intern's genuine experience:
- **Concrete Technical Context (5 pts)**:
  Describes specific tools, platforms, bug fixes, or hardware configurations encountered at the host organisation rather than generic textbook definitions.
- **Realistic Troubleshooting & Recommendations (5 pts)**:
  Presents authentic challenge-and-solution pairs (e.g. multi-service container communication, database query optimization) and constructive recommendations tailored specifically to the host company, university, and ITF.
  *Deductions*: -3 pts for generic textbook platitudes; -5 pts for unsubstantiated claims of grand corporate restructuring.

## 3. Score Breakdown & Performance Tiers

| Score Range | Classification | Academic Verdict |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| **90 – 100** | **Grade A (Distinction)** | **Approved for binding & defense.** High technical depth, zero fabrications, flawless layout. |
| **80 – 89** | **Grade B (Good)** | **Minor revisions required.** Requires minor copyediting or formatting alignment. |
| **70 – 79** | **Grade C (Satisfactory)** | **Major revisions required.** Thin technical detail or page length mismatch. |
| **< 70** | **Fail (Unsatisfactory)** | **Rejected.** Fails department criteria; must be rewritten and re-evaluated. |
| **0** | **AUTOMATIC FAIL** | **Disqualified.** Fabricated claims or cross-tenant leakage detected. |

## 4. Scorecard Invocation & CLI Tooling

The scorecard can be executed on any generated report, project, or against the test golden set:

```bash
# Evaluate a specific project
python backend/manage.py evaluate_scorecard --project-id 31

# Evaluate a specific report
python backend/manage.py evaluate_scorecard --report-id 10

# Run full Golden Set validation (fails CI if any score < 90 or fabrication occurs)
python backend/manage.py evaluate_scorecard --golden-set

# Export human review package (PDFs + scorecard reports for coordinator review)
python backend/manage.py evaluate_scorecard --human-review
```

## 5. What the Scorecard Cannot Judge (Manual Coordinator Review Checklist)
While the automated scorecard and AI reviewer evaluate structural, linguistic, and factual compliance with high fidelity, certain physical and academic dimensions remain within the domain of the academic coordinator:
1. **Physical Supervisor Ink Signatures**: Verifying that ink signatures and corporate stamps on physical logbooks match registered industry supervisors.
2. **Real-world Absence Context**: Situations where a student physically attended a workplace but performed unlogged observation, or where tasks were performed off-site during power outages.
3. **Oral Defense Mastery**: The student's ability to verbally explain architecture diagrams, code patterns, or hardware components during the oral defense panel.
